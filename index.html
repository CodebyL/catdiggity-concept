Good questions.

How long does grooming take?

Smaller dogs usually take about 2 hours and larger dogs about 4 hours, but timing varies. Please pick up within an hour of the salon's text or call.

How do I book?

The salon works by appointment. Book online or call (516) 396-9310. If you have a favorite groomer, mention it when you book.

How can I pay?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, Android Pay and cash. You pay after the grooming is done.

What if my dog is hard to groom?

The salon never uses sedatives or drugs. If a pet moves too much to groom safely, the groomers stop and call you to pick up.
